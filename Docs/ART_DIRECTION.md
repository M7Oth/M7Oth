# Art Direction

## Visual identity
- **Grounded modern realism**, Gulf-coast city: warm sandstone, glass and steel towers, palm-lined boulevard, souq arches.
- Default lighting: late golden hour → blue hour (long shadows, warm sun, cool skylight). Night variant planned with sodium street lights and police light bars as a key visual.
- Readability over grit: no heavy bloom, motion blur off by default, filmic but neutral grade (slight teal shadows, warm highlights).

## Team readability (competitive clarity)
| | Police | Robbers |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | navy uniform, hi-vis yellow trims, blue HUD | civilian earth tones + **amber** HUD accent, masks |
| Silhouette | caps/helmets, vests, duty belts | hoodies/jackets, duffel bags, masks |
| UI colour | `#2F7BFF` | `#FFB020` |
Colour-blind option (setting exists) swaps to blue/orange-safe pair with shape icons.

## Materials
- PBR, physically plausible albedo (no pure black/white), roughness variation via grunge masks, decals for road wear, oil stains, cracked pavement, posters.
- Glass: real reflections via Lumen; interiors behind glass use interior-mapping materials for non-explorable buildings.
- Vehicles: clear-coat car paint, livery decals for police.

## Location identities
- Bank: marble lobby, brass details, cold steel vault — "old money".
- Police HQ: functional concrete + blue signage.
- Souq: warm lanterns, fabric awnings, narrow shade.
- Harbour: containers, cranes, sodium lights, wet concrete.

## VFX & feedback
Muzzle flash/tracers/impacts (Niagara, restrained), smoke grenade volumetric, alarm strobes in bank, siren lights, drill sparks, money bag dust. No blood/gore.

## Benchmarks
Target quality bar: modern AA (e.g., City Sample-level architecture density, Lyra-level character readability).
